玩家支持

iPhone 版本准备中，尚未正式上架。游戏内文字仅为繁体中文；本网站和商店介绍提供三种语言。

从哪里开始？

在首页选择“開始遊戲”，依次选择时代与君主，进入战役。选中己方城池后，可以管理武将、准备兵力并出征。战斗中可以下达军令、让主将出击或停止前进，并使用武将技。

游戏支持哪些语言？

游戏内界面与文字仅支持繁体中文。商店提供简体中文、繁体中文和英文介绍，方便了解玩法。

免费体验如何计算？

本局累计新占领两座不同城市后，需要一次性购买完整战役解锁。初始城市不计入；失去后再夺回同一座城市不会重复计数。第二座城池的战斗与结算会先完成，再出现解锁提示。购买不是订阅，没有自动续费。

已经购买，如何恢复？

使用原来购买时的 App Store 账号，保持联网，在游戏的“完整遊戲 / 恢復購買”入口选择恢复购买。恢复既有购买不会再次收费。Apple 与 Google Play 的购买权益不互通。

解锁后还需要联网吗？

购买与权益验证需要联网。设备会保存有期限的验证凭证；凭证过期且设备离线时，请联网后恢复或刷新购买。不要重复付款来处理验证问题。

为什么换设备后没有原来的进度？

购买权益与游戏存档是两件事。恢复购买不会同步战役存档。当前进度保存在设备本地，删除应用可能删除本地进度；操作前请确认自己的备份。

Player support

The iPhone release is in preparation and is not yet on the App Store. In-game text is Traditional Chinese only. This website and store descriptions are available in three languages.

Getting started

Select 開始遊戲 on the title screen, choose an era and a ruler, then start your campaign. Select one of your cities to manage officers, prepare troops and set out. In battle, issue troop orders, send your general forward or stop their advance, and use character skills.

In-game language

The game's interface and text are available in Traditional Chinese only. Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English descriptions are provided on the store listing.

Free play and the full-campaign unlock

After capturing two distinct new cities in one campaign, a one-time purchase is required to continue the full campaign. Initial cities do not count, and recapturing the same city does not count twice. The second city's battle and settlement finish before the unlock prompt appears. This purchase is not a subscription and does not renew automatically.

Restoring a purchase

Connect to the internet, use the App Store account that made the purchase, and select 恢復購買 from the 完整遊戲 / 恢復購買 entry. Restoring an existing purchase does not charge you again. Apple and Google Play purchases are separate.

Internet access after unlocking

Purchases and entitlement verification require an internet connection. The device caches a time-limited verification ticket. If it expires while you are offline, reconnect and restore or refresh your purchase. Do not purchase again to resolve a verification error.

Moving to a different device

A purchase entitlement and a campaign save are separate. Restoring a purchase does not transfer campaign saves. Progress is stored on the device; deleting the app may remove local saves. Check your backup before deleting it.

Contact support

Email feixuwu@gmail.com with your device model, operating system version, game version, the steps leading to the problem and any error message. You may attach a screenshot without personal information. Never send your Apple ID password, payment-card information or a full payment receipt.
