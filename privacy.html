隐私说明

本说明适用于《三国群豪传》iPhone 版本及本网站。游戏正在准备发行；iPhone 购买权益使用 Apple StoreKit 2 验证，不使用开发者自建的收据验证服务器。更新日期：2026-10-25。

游戏进度与本地数据

无需注册游戏账号。战役进度、设置和购买状态缓存保存在设备上，当前没有开发者运营的云存档。恢复购买不会恢复战役进度。系统备份与恢复受 Apple 的相关设置和规则管理；删除应用可能删除本地进度。

购买与权益验证

iPhone 版本通过 App Store 提供一次性完整战役解锁及恢复购买。Apple 处理支付和商店交易记录；游戏在设备上读取并验证 Apple 提供的购买权益，仅接受有效且未被撤销的完整游戏购买。游戏不向开发者自建服务器发送收据、交易标识或安装标识，也不设置自定义的 30 天离线解锁期限。购买及恢复需要连接 Apple 服务，退款或撤销会在权益刷新时反映。开发者无法获知你的 Apple 账户密码或付款卡号。

网站、客服与诊断

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，没有额外的广告或访问统计脚本。GitHub 可能接收 IP 地址和常规访问日志，并按其隐私声明处理。主动发送支持邮件时，开发者及邮件服务商会处理你提供的邮箱、问题描述和附件，用于回复与排查。请勿发送密码、完整支付凭据或不必要的个人信息。开发预览可能在设备上生成诊断日志；如果你主动提供日志，请先检查其中的内容。

数据使用、保留与删除

当前游戏业务未接入广告投放或跨应用广告追踪。我们不将购买凭据用于广告。设备本地数据保留到用户删除、清除应用数据或卸载，系统备份另按平台规则处理。客服邮件仅在处理请求和适用法律义务所需期间保留。Apple 交易记录、GitHub 日志及邮件平台的数据按各自政策处理；删除应用不会删除这些服务方的记录。

你的选择与联系我们

可通过客服邮箱提出有关已向开发者提供的信息的查询、更正或删除请求。我们会根据适用规则处理，并在需要时核实请求身份。家长若认为儿童向开发者提供了个人信息，也可联系此邮箱。数据处理发生变化时，将更新本页和日期；正式上线前会完成实际服务和最终应用组件的核对。

Privacy

This notice covers the iPhone version of Three Kingdoms: Heroic Tales and this website. The game is being prepared for release. iPhone purchase entitlements are verified with Apple StoreKit 2, without a developer-operated receipt-verification server. Updated October 25, 2026.

Campaigns and local data

No game account is required. Campaign progress, settings and purchase-state caches are stored on your device. There is currently no developer-operated cloud-save service. Restoring purchases does not restore campaign saves. Operating-system backup and restoration follow Apple settings and rules. Removing the app may remove local progress.

Purchases and entitlement verification

The iPhone version offers a one-time full-campaign unlock and purchase restoration through the App Store. Apple handles payments and store transaction records. The game reads and verifies Apple-provided entitlements on the device and accepts only valid, non-revoked full-game purchases. It does not send receipts, transaction identifiers or installation identifiers to a developer-operated server, or impose a custom 30-day offline unlock limit. Purchase and restoration connect to Apple services. Refunds and revocations take effect when entitlement information refreshes. The developer does not receive your Apple Account password or payment-card number.

Website, support and diagnostics

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and has no additional advertising or visitor-analytics scripts. GitHub may receive IP addresses and routine access logs under its privacy statement. If you email support, the developer and email providers process the email address, issue description and attachments you choose to send in order to respond and investigate. Do not send passwords, full payment credentials or unnecessary personal information. Development previews may create diagnostic logs on the device; review logs before voluntarily sharing them.

Use, retention and deletion

The current game business implementation does not integrate advertising delivery or cross-app advertising tracking. We do not use purchase proof for advertising. Local data remains until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall, subject to separate operating-system backup behavior. Support emails are retained only as needed to handle your request and applicable legal obligations. Apple transaction records, GitHub logs and email-provider data follow those providers’ policies. Removing the game does not remove provider-held records.

Your choices and contact

Use the support email to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have provided directly to the developer. Requests are handled under applicable rules and may require identity verification. Parents or guardians may also contact us about personal information a child has sent. Material processing changes will be reflected on this page and its update date. Actual services and final app components will be checked before launch.
