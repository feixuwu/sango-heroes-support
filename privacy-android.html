本機進度與帳號

遊戲不要求註冊開發者帳號。戰役存檔、設定、購買憑據快取及解鎖票據保存在裝置上，沒有開發者營運的雲端存檔。恢復購買僅找回已購資格，不恢復戰役進度。清除資料或解除安裝可能刪除本機資料；Android 系統備份另按系統與 Google 設定處理。

Google Play 購買與驗證

遊戲免費下載，單局新占領兩座不同的非初始城市後，須一次性購買完整戰役。付款由 Google Play 處理，開發者無法取得 Google 密碼或付款卡號。購買、啟動時的權益更新及恢復購買會透過 HTTPS 向開發者在 us.adsshare.org 的驗證服務傳送商品與應用程式識別碼、Google 提供的購買憑據（包括購買權杖及交易識別碼），以及裝置上隨機產生的安裝 UUID。UUID 不是廣告 ID、IMEI 或硬體序號。

這些資料僅用於查詢 Google Play 的購買狀態、確認永久商品訂單、避免無效或退款訂單解鎖，並簽發綁定此安裝的解鎖票據。購買權杖會送往 Google 的 Android Publisher API。驗證服務在記憶體中處理請求，不建立玩家帳號或購買資料庫，不將權杖、憑據、交易識別碼或 UUID 寫入應用日誌；驗證路徑不保留存取日誌。伺服器在連線時會短暫接收 IP 位址，僅供傳輸與短期流量限制，不用於推算位置或廣告。

解鎖票據及憑據快取保存在本機。正式版離線票據最長 30 天，內測票據最長 1 天；票據期限不是再次收費或訂閱期限。重新連線時會重查商店資格；退款或撤銷後可能失去完整版資格。Google 按其政策保留商店交易資料。遊戲不出售資料，不以此資料進行廣告、行銷或跨應用追蹤。

客服、網站與刪除

主動寄送客服郵件時，開發者及郵件服務商會處理你提供的信箱、問題及附件，用於回覆和排查，僅在處理請求及適用義務所需期間保留。請勿提供密碼、完整購買權杖或付款卡號。本支援網站由 GitHub Pages 託管，沒有額外的廣告或流量統計指令碼；GitHub 可能依其隱私聲明保留網站存取資料。開發者未接入遊戲廣告或額外的行為分析服務。

可在裝置清除本機遊戲資料，或聯絡 feixuwu@gmail.com 請求查詢、更正或刪除你曾主動提供給開發者的資訊。清除本機資料不會刪除 Google 的交易記錄；Google 帳號與交易資料請依 Google 的工具和政策管理。遊戲內沒有開發者帳號可刪除。家長若認為兒童提供了個人資訊，也可聯絡此信箱。

Google Play Games 元件與審核存取

本應用包含 Google Play Games Services 元件。依 Google 的說明，該元件可能自動傳送分析與診斷資料，以改善元件穩定性及產品；若使用者建立或更新 Play Games 帳戶，Google 也會處理玩家代號及頭像。這些資料依 Google 的隱私政策處理，可透過 Play Games 個人資料或 Google 帳戶工具管理和刪除。本遊戲未實作雲端戰役存檔、成就、排行榜或好友清單功能。

經授權的審核人員可輸入專用存取代碼，透過同一 HTTPS 服務驗證資格並取得短期解鎖票據。代碼僅用於存取驗證，不作為付款憑據；伺服器不記錄明文代碼。

Google Play Games Services may automatically collect analytics and diagnostics to improve SDK stability and products. Google may process gamer identity when a Games account is created or updated. Manage/delete this data through your Play Games profile or Google Account. This game does not implement cloud campaign saves, achievements, leaderboards or friend lists. Authorized reviewers may submit an access code over HTTPS to obtain short-lived entitlement tickets; the service does not log the code.

English summary

This policy applies to the Google Play edition of Three Kingdoms: Heroic Tales (三國群豪傳), package com.feixuwu.sangoheroes. Developer/contact: feixuwu, feixuwu@gmail.com. Updated October 31, 2026.

No developer account or developer-operated cloud saves are provided. Saves, preferences, purchase proof and entitlement tickets remain on the device. Restoring purchases restores ownership, not saved progress. Google Play handles payment; we do not receive your Google password or card number.

For purchases and ownership refresh/restore, the game sends purchase proof (including the Google purchase token and transaction identifier), product/application IDs and a randomly generated installation UUID over HTTPS to the developer's verification service at us.adsshare.org. The service queries Google's Android Publisher API, acknowledges valid permanent-product orders and signs installation-bound entitlement tickets. It processes requests in memory, maintains no player/purchase database and does not log purchase tokens, receipts, transaction IDs or UUIDs. Access logging is disabled for the verification path. IP addresses are temporarily processed for network delivery and short-term rate limiting, not location inference or advertising. Production offline tickets last up to 30 days (internal-test tickets up to one day); this is not a recurring fee. Refunds/revocations can remove entitlement on refresh. Google retains transactions under its policies.

No advertising, sale of data, cross-app tracking or developer-added behavioral analytics is used. Voluntary support emails and attachments are processed by the developer and email provider only as needed to handle support and applicable obligations. This website uses GitHub Pages without added analytics/ads; GitHub may process website access data. Clear app data to remove local data, or email feixuwu@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests concerning information you supplied to the developer. Google account and transaction data are managed through Google's tools/policies. Uninstalling does not delete Google's purchase records. Parents may contact the same address about children's information.
